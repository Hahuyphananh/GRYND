# Neon Flush (UNO) — Game Guide

Neon Flush is GRYND's UNO-style card game, available **solo (vs AI)** and **1v1 online**. Classic UNO rules: match the top card by colour or value, use action cards to mess with your opponent, and be the first to empty your hand to win the round.

## How a round works

1. **Deal** — each player is dealt a hand; a starting card is flipped onto the pile.
2. **Play a card** — on your turn, play a card that matches the top card's **colour or value**:
   - **Number cards** — play by matching colour or number.
   - **Skip** — skip the opponent's next turn.
   - **Reverse** — reverse the turn order.
   - **Draw Two (+2)** — the opponent draws 2 and loses their turn.
   - **Wild** — change the current colour to anything you choose.
   - **Wild Draw Four (+4)** — change the colour AND the opponent draws 4 (classic UNO rules apply).
   - If you can't play, draw a card — you may play it immediately if it matches.
3. **Round end** — the first player to empty their hand wins the round.

## Match format

- **Solo (vs AI)** — play rounds against the GRYND AI.
- **1v1 online** — real-time rounds against another player. Rounds accumulate (rounds-won tallies) and players can toggle "skip next round" between rounds.
- Nothing is staked and no pot is paid: the result is worth trophies and rating only.

## Modes

- **Neon Flush Solo** — free practice vs AI.
- **Neon Flush 1v1 Online** — head-to-head rounds against another player.
