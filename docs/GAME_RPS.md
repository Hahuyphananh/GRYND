# Rock Paper Scissors (RPS) — Game Guide

RPS on GRYND is a **best-of-7** duel: you and your opponent pick rock, paper, or scissors simultaneously every round — no turn-taking, no seeing the other's pick first — and the first player to win 4 rounds takes the pot.

## How a match works

1. **Mode** — play the AI (free practice) or a staked PvP match.
2. **Each round** — both players pick **Rock**, **Paper**, or **Scissors** at the same time:
   - Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
   - A matching pick is a tie (the round is replayed).
3. **Best of 7** — the first player to **4 round wins** takes the pot.
4. **Forfeit** — a player can forfeit mid-match; the forfeiter loses and the opponent wins the pot minus the house rake.

## Payout

- **Winner** — takes the pot minus the 5% PvP rake.
- **Loser** — loses the bet.
- **Draw** — both refunded.

## Modes

- **RPS vs AI** — free practice against the GRYND AI.
- **RPS PvP** — staked best-of-7 duel against another player.

## Key numbers

| Constant | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Match format | Best of 7 (first to 4) |
| PvP rake | 5% of the pot |
